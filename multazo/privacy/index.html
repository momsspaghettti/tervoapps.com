Privacy Policy for Multazo

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Multazo ("Multazo: Team Fines & Subs" on the App Store) is an app for the treasurer of an amateur sports team. It keeps track of fines, fees, payments and expenses and shows how much is still open for each player. Players pay the treasurer directly, for example in cash, by bank transfer or with PayPal. Multazo never handles money and does not connect to any bank or payment account.

This policy explains what happens to the information in Multazo. It is written for treasurers and for players.

In short

  • You don't create an account with us, and we have no server. We do not receive what you enter in Multazo, and we cannot see it.
  • There is no analytics or tracking in the app, and no advertising. Apple gives us download, sales and subscription reports, and crash reports and usage statistics if you agreed to share them with app developers. None of these include your name, email address or payment details.
  • The teams you create are stored on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account, which Apple runs. A team that someone shares with you stays in their iCloud.
  • When a treasurer shares a team, everyone they let in receives all of the team's entries on their devices, along with the names and Apple Account email addresses of everyone in the team.
  • Messages, images and files leave Multazo only when you send them yourself. A statement sent as a link is first stored in your iCloud.

What Multazo stores

Multazo stores your teams in a database on your device:

  • team details, such as the name, sport, country, currency and cash goal;
  • players: the name or nickname the treasurer enters;
  • the fines catalog and every entry: fines, fees, payments, expenses and corrections, each with its date, amount and an optional note;
  • the dates on which the treasurer sent each player a reminder, and which entries were already announced in a team summary;
  • the treasurer's payment details for players: IBAN, account holder's name, payment reference, PayPal username, payment link and any other payment methods the treasurer types in, such as a Bizum number;
  • players' phone numbers and private notes about players, which only the treasurer has;
  • your settings, such as the training days used for reminders.

Each entry and each reminder also carries a random code that Multazo creates when it is installed on a device. It is not derived from your name, your Apple Account or your device, and everyone in a shared team can receive it. Multazo uses it to tell which device recorded something, for example to let you know when an entry you made was not saved.

If you are signed in to iCloud, Multazo syncs your teams through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to your other devices with the same Apple Account. The teams you create are then stored in your iCloud account and count toward your iCloud storage. If you are not signed in, they stay on the device.

If you use Multazo as a player, your iCloud also remembers which player in the team you are, so that your other devices know it.

When you sign out of iCloud or switch to another Apple Account, Multazo removes that account's teams from the device; they stay in iCloud. Before that, it saves a recovery copy on the device with the entries that were not uploaded yet and a copy of every team you own, including players' phone numbers and private notes. If you sign in again with the same account, Multazo puts the missing entries back and deletes the copy. If someone signs in with a different account on the device, the copy stays until it is restored there as a new team or deleted in Multazo. Changes to existing entries that were not uploaded can still be lost.

We receive none of this. Multazo does not send your data to us. It goes only to your own iCloud and, if a team is shared, to the people in it. We have no server, and we have no access to the data in your iCloud.

Permissions

  • Contacts: to add players from your contacts, iOS shows its contact picker. Multazo receives only the contacts you pick and keeps the full name and one phone number of each. It has no access to the rest of your address book.
  • Photos: Multazo can save an image to your photo library when you ask it to. It cannot see your photos. You can withdraw this permission at any time in the Settings app, under Multazo.
  • Notifications: Multazo asks before it sends any (see Notifications).

If you are a treasurer

When you use Multazo, you enter information about other people: your players' names, perhaps their phone numbers, and what they were charged and paid. You decide what to enter and who to share it with. This data never reaches us, so we cannot see, change or delete it for you. Where data protection law applies to the way you or your club keep the team cash, you or your club are responsible for this data.

Some practical points:

  • A first name or a nickname is enough. Multazo takes the full name from your contacts, and you can shorten it.
  • Tell your team that you keep the cash in Multazo, for example by sending them a link to this page. This page explains the app; it does not replace any information your club owes its members.
  • Don't put health details or other sensitive information into notes. Notes on entries are seen by everyone you share the team with. Private notes about a player stay with you, on your devices and in your iCloud.
  • If a player asks you to correct or remove their data, you can rename them or delete them (see How long data is kept).

If you are a player

Your treasurer, not us, decides what is recorded about you in Multazo. We can't see your team's data, so please ask your treasurer if you want to see it or have it corrected or removed.

When a treasurer deletes a player, Multazo replaces the name with "Former player" and a number, deletes the phone number and private notes, and clears the notes on that player's entries. The amounts stay, so that the team's cash total stays correct.

If the treasurer lets you into the team in the app, the next section applies to you too.

Sharing a team

A shared team is stored in the iCloud account of the treasurer who created it. The people in it see it through iCloud sharing.

Inviting players. The treasurer sends an invitation link, for example to the team chat. Anyone who opens the link can ask to join, and iOS tells them that the treasurer will see their email address. In Multazo the treasurer then sees the name and the email address or phone number of the Apple Account that asks, and approves or declines each request. Only approved people get in, and they can only view the team. Once someone is in, everyone else in the team receives their name and email address too (see below). Treasurers: approve only people you know.

What everyone in a shared team receives. Everyone in a shared team gets the whole team on their devices: all players' names, all entries with their amounts and notes, the reminder dates, the team's settings and the treasurer's payment details. This is true even when Multazo shows a player only their own balance: the setting "Players see the whole table" changes what the app shows, not what is stored on players' devices. Players' phone numbers and private notes about players are never shared.

Names and email addresses. iCloud keeps the list of people in a shared team, with the name and the email address or phone number of each person's Apple Account, the treasurer's included. This list is part of the share, so it reaches the devices of everyone in the team, players included. In Multazo, players see only the treasurer's name. The treasurer sees the names and email addresses of the people who have access and of those asking to join, and the journal shows who recorded an entry. Multazo reads these details from iCloud when it shows them and keeps no copy in its own data, files or backups. iCloud also marks each entry with an internal ID of the Apple Account that created it.

Co-treasurer (Pro). A treasurer can invite one co-treasurer, who can also record and correct entries, add players and edit the fines catalog. To invite them, the treasurer types the email address of the co-treasurer's Apple Account. Multazo uses it only to find that person in iCloud and does not keep it itself; iCloud keeps it as part of the share.

Leaving and removing. A player or co-treasurer can leave a team in Multazo, which removes the team from their devices. The treasurer can remove anyone's access. What the treasurer recorded about a player stays in the team.

Messages, summaries and files you send

Multazo prepares texts, images and files. You send them yourself, to whoever you choose, through the iOS share sheet or directly in WhatsApp or Messages:

  • a reminder to one player: their name, what is still open and how to pay (IBAN, PayPal; with Pro also a payment link with the amount and a QR code for banking apps). For a reminder by WhatsApp or SMS, Multazo hands the player's phone number and the text to WhatsApp or to the iOS message window;
  • a summary for the team chat, as an image and text: players' names and amounts, and the reasons if you turn them on;
  • with Pro: the "Top sponsors" image (the players who paid in the most, usually five and more if tied, what each paid in, and the team's total), a PDF statement for one player, and a cash report PDF for the team meeting or the cash audit, which has amounts per player but no notes, phone numbers or payment details;
  • for players: a message telling the treasurer that you have paid, and a recommendation of Multazo to a treasurer.

Once sent, these are handled by the app or service you used, under its own privacy policy, and Multazo cannot call them back. We never receive a copy.

The pay buttons in a player's view open PayPal or let the player copy the IBAN. What happens there is between the player and PayPal or their bank.

Statements by link (Pro)

With Pro, a treasurer can send a player their statement as a link instead of a file. About these links:

  • The PDF is stored by Apple in the iCloud of the treasurer who made it.
  • Anyone who has the link can open and download the PDF for 30 days after it was made, at least until the date shown in the message. That includes anyone the player forwards it to. No Apple Account is needed to open it.
  • The treasurer cannot withdraw the link before then. Deleting the player, the team or all data deletes the PDF from the treasurer's iCloud, but a link that was already sent may still open until its date.
  • The PDF contains the team's name, the player's name, their open fines and amounts, and the payment details the treasurer entered: IBAN, account holder's name, payment reference, PayPal username, payment link and any other payment methods typed in (for example a Bizum number), plus a QR code for banking apps if the team has it turned on. It contains no player's phone number and no notes.
  • The file name (team, player, date) is part of the link, so anyone who sees the link in a chat can read it without opening it.
  • The download page shows the file name, not the treasurer's name or Apple Account. The link also contains the name of Multazo's storage area in iCloud.
  • 31 days after it was made, Multazo deletes the PDF from the iCloud of the treasurer who made it, the next time Multazo is opened on one of their devices.

A treasurer can also send the statement as a file. It is then like any other file you send.

Notifications

  • Reminders for the treasurer, such as "any fines today?" on training days or a reminder to make a backup at the end of the month, are scheduled on your device.
  • In a shared team, Multazo can tell you that there are new entries, and tell the treasurer when something changes in who can see the team. These notifications are sent by Apple's iCloud through Apple's push service. Their text is fixed and contains no names or amounts.
  • Multazo asks for your permission first. You can turn notifications off at any time in the Settings app.

We have no server that sends notifications, and we never receive your device's push token.

Widgets

The player widget shows your own balance. The treasurer widget (Pro) shows the team's cash, what is still open, and up to three players with the most open. Widgets read a small file that Multazo keeps on your device; nothing is sent anywhere. Anyone who can see your screen can read what a widget shows. Multazo clears this file when you leave a team, delete all data or switch iCloud accounts.

Exports and backups

A treasurer can export the journal as a CSV file (names, entries, amounts and notes) and make a backup file of a team. A backup made by the treasurer contains the whole team, including players' phone numbers and private notes. It does not contain the names or email addresses of the people the team is shared with: for each entry it only says whether the treasurer or a co-treasurer recorded it. These files have no password. You decide where to save or send them, so keep them somewhere safe. Importing a backup creates a new team.

This stays on your device. If you choose to record the price of Pro as a team expense, that entry becomes part of the team, and everyone the team is shared with sees it. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store account settings.

Pro works on the devices of the Apple Account that bought it. Players don't need Pro.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Data in Multazo stays until you delete it:

  • Delete a player (treasurer): the name is replaced by "Former player" and a number; the phone number, private notes and the notes on that player's entries are removed; their statement PDFs are deleted. Archiving a player instead keeps the name.
  • Delete a team (treasurer): removes the team, its sharing and its statement PDFs from all devices and from iCloud, so everyone in it loses it. Multazo offers to export it first.
  • Leave a team (player or co-treasurer): removes the team from your devices.
  • Delete all data (in Multazo's settings): deletes every team you own, with its sharing and statement PDFs, leaves every team you joined, and clears Multazo's data on your device.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from that device, but not from iCloud or from your other devices.

A deletion reaches your other devices and the devices of the people in a shared team when Multazo next runs there and syncs. Statement PDFs are deleted from the iCloud of the person who made them, the next time Multazo runs on one of their devices.

Some things are outside Multazo's reach: messages, images and files you have already sent; statement links already sent, until their date; exports and backups you have saved; and backups of your device made by iCloud Backup or your computer, which can include Multazo's data until they are replaced.

Children

Multazo is made for adults who keep the cash of an adult team. It is not directed at children, and we receive no data from the app about anyone, children included. If you keep the cash of a youth team, you are recording data about children and teenagers yourself: enter as little as possible, and involve their parents where that is needed. If you think a child's data has reached us, for example by email, write to us and we will delete it.

You can delete what Multazo keeps on your own devices and in your iCloud, and leave a team, at any time. What a treasurer recorded about you stays until the treasurer changes or deletes it.

For emails you have sent us, write to support@tervoapps.com. We answer without undue delay and within one month at the latest. We make no automated decisions about you.

In the UK, it is the Information Commissioner's Office.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising.
