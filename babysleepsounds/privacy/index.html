Privacy Policy — Baby Sleep Sounds

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Baby Sleep Sounds is made by Tervo. We designed it to keep your information private: the app does not collect or share your personal data, and it sends nothing you do in it to us or to anyone else, apart from purchases and ratings, which go to Apple. The only data the app keeps is stored locally on your device, under your control.

This policy applies to everyone who uses Baby Sleep Sounds, worldwide. If you have any questions about it, contact us at support@tervoapps.com.

Data the app collects

None. Baby Sleep Sounds does not collect personal data. There is no account and no sign-up. We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or any third-party SDKs. The app has no backend server of ours, so there is nowhere for the app to send your data. Personal data reaches us only if you email us (see If you write to us) and possibly through Apple's App Store reports (see Purchases). When you visit this website, our web host logs your IP address (see This website).

Data stored on your device

The app saves a few things locally on your device so it can work the way you expect:

  • your app preferences and onboarding state,
  • the sound mixes you create and save,
  • a few on-device counters that decide when to show the optional "rate the app" prompt (for example, how many days you've played sounds),
  • your purchase status (whether you have unlocked premium).

This information never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us. Because it lives only on your device, you are in full control of it: there is no consent for us to manage and nothing for us to retain or delete on a server. You can erase all of it at any time by deleting the app. (If you back up your device with iCloud or your computer, that backup is created and handled by Apple and may include the app's data; we never have access to it.)

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment and acts as the data controller for the information involved in the transaction — we never see or store your payment details. Apple gives us download statistics and sales and subscription reports, for example the country, device type and date of a purchase, under a random ID that Apple assigns to you and that is the same in all our apps. If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple also sends us crash reports and usage statistics. None of these include your name, email address or payment details, and Apple says its statistics do not identify you. If any of this still counts as personal data, we use it only to keep track of sales, fix errors and improve the app, based on our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), and for our bookkeeping, based on our obligations under Spanish tax and accounting law (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR). Apple keeps the sales and subscription reports in App Store Connect for one year (yearly reports for ten years) and decides how long crash reports and usage statistics stay available. We download some of Apple's sales and subscription reports for our bookkeeping and keep them only as long as Spanish tax and accounting rules require. The app itself only checks your plan (which one, whether it is a free trial, when it renews or ends, and whether you can still get the free trial), so it can unlock premium features and show your plan in Settings. It never learns your name, email address or payment method. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your App Store account settings. Apple's handling of your information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Tracking and advertising

We do not track you across apps or websites, and the app does not use the device advertising identifier (IDFA). There are no ads. We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising, and we have not done so in the past 12 months.

If you write to us

If you email support@tervoapps.com, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and what you write, including any attachments and the usual technical details of an email, such as when it was sent. We use this only to answer you and deal with your request. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message is about using Baby Sleep Sounds, and otherwise our legitimate interest in answering messages (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You don't have to write to us, but we need your email address to reply.

Our address is an email forwarding service of our domain registrar, Porkbun LLC, in the United States. Porkbun handles your email on our behalf and only to pass it on. This transfer to the United States is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses in Porkbun's data processing addendum; you can ask us for a copy. Porkbun passes the email on to our Gmail mailbox, which Google Ireland Limited provides. Google acts there as a separate controller under its own terms and privacy policy, not on our instructions. Google may process emails on servers outside the EU, including in the United States, and relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses for this.

We delete emails once we no longer need them, at the latest two years after our last exchange, unless we need them for a legal claim or Spanish tax or commercial law requires us to keep them longer. You can object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interest (see Your privacy rights).

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open a page, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, as it describes in its documentation. We do not receive these logs. GitHub decides how long it keeps them, under the criteria in its Privacy Statement. We use GitHub Pages because of our legitimate interest in running a simple and secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub states in its Privacy Statement that it complies with the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

Children's privacy

Baby Sleep Sounds is intended for parents and other adults and is not directed to children. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from a child under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at support@tervoapps.com and we will address it promptly.

Your privacy rights

Privacy laws such as the EU/EEA and UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), among others, give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you, for example to access, correct, delete or transfer it, or to restrict how it is used. Because Baby Sleep Sounds collects no personal data, we hold nothing about you from the app. The Apple reports we keep (see Purchases) identify you only by Apple's random ID, without your name or email address, so we cannot find you in them (Article 11 GDPR). If you have emailed us, you can use these rights for your emails by writing to support@tervoapps.com. We answer without undue delay and within one month at the latest.

Right to object. Where we rely on our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, by writing to support@tervoapps.com (Article 21 GDPR).

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority or with the Spanish authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos. If you have any questions, you can always reach us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we will update the date at the top and, where appropriate, note them in an app update.
